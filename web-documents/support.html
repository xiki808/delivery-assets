IOweTea Create app support

IOweTea Create lets you follow and respond to software development tasks from your phone. The app connects to the IOweTea Create dashboard at create.iowetea.com.

Get help

Email create.support@iowetea.com. Include your device type, app version, and a short description of the problem. Please do not send passwords, API keys, or private task content by email.

Account access

An IOweTea Create account is needed to use the dashboard. If you need access or cannot sign in, contact the support address above. The app does not currently offer self-service sign-up.

Notifications

When available on your account, you can manage task notifications on the dashboard's Account page. You can also change notification permission in your phone settings.

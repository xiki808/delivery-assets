Delivery app support

Delivery lets you follow and respond to software development tasks from your phone. The app connects to the Delivery dashboard at delivery.iowetea.com.

Get help

Email info.iowetea@gmail.com. Include your device type, app version, and a short description of the problem. Please do not send passwords, API keys, or private task content by email.

Account access

A Delivery account is needed to use the dashboard. If you need access or cannot sign in, contact the support address above. The app does not currently offer self-service sign-up.

Notifications

When available on your account, you can manage task notifications on the dashboard's Account page. You can also change notification permission in your phone settings.

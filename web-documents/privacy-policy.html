Delivery privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy describes the Delivery mobile app and the Delivery dashboard it opens. For questions about this policy or your data, contact info.iowetea@gmail.com.

Information used by Delivery

To provide an account and dashboard, Delivery processes your account email and profile, sign-in sessions, connected machine and repository details, tasks, messages, and files you choose to upload. Tasks and messages may contain personal or confidential information that you enter.

If you enable phone notifications, the app obtains a push token from Firebase Cloud Messaging and sends that token, a device identifier, and the platform type to the Delivery service. Delivery uses these to address task notifications to your phone. Notifications may contain a task title and identifier. Notification content can appear on a locked screen according to your device settings. You can turn notifications off in the dashboard Account page or your device settings.

The mobile app stores notification preferences and a push token on the device. Its embedded browser uses the Delivery dashboard and its sign-in session. The app can open the system file picker when you choose an attachment.

How information is used and shared

Delivery uses account and task data to show the dashboard, coordinate work with your connected machines, and send the task notifications you request. Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service, carries push messages. Your chosen repository, hosting, and AI agent providers may receive information when you connect or use those services through Delivery. Their own terms and privacy policies also apply.

Storage and security

Delivery stores service data on its backend and sends it between the dashboard, backend, and your connected machines as needed for the service. Connections to the hosted dashboard use HTTPS. Access to account data requires sign-in. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion

You can disable phone notifications and sign out of the app. To delete your account, sign in and use Account → Delete account; you will confirm your email and password before permanent deletion. If you cannot sign in, use the email route in the account deletion instructions. To request access, correction, or export, email the address above. Deleting Delivery data does not automatically remove copies held on your machines or in external provider accounts.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

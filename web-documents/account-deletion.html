Delete a Delivery account

To request deletion of your Delivery account and associated cloud data, email info.iowetea@gmail.com from the email address on your account. Put “Delivery account deletion request” in the subject. If you cannot use that email address, contact us and we will arrange another way to verify ownership.

We will confirm your identity before deleting an account. Account deletion removes access to the Delivery dashboard. It may also affect connected development machines, task history, project data, and registered notification devices. Ask us if you need an export or help disconnecting a machine before deletion.

We will explain what we can delete and any records we must retain before completing the request. Data stored on your own machines or in external services, such as repository and agent providers, may need to be removed separately.

Uninstalling the mobile app or switching off notifications does not delete your account.

Delete a Delivery account

If you can sign in, open Delivery → Account, select Delete account, and confirm your account email and password. The action permanently removes your Delivery account and associated data on Delivery's server. You will be signed out.

If you cannot sign in or have already uninstalled the app, request deletion by emailing info.iowetea@gmail.com from the email address on your account. Put “Delivery account deletion request” in the subject. If you cannot use that address, contact us to arrange another way to verify ownership. We will confirm when manual deletion is complete.

Account deletion removes dashboard access, task history, uploaded files, connected machine records, and registered notification devices from Delivery's server. Ask us if you need an export or help disconnecting a machine before deletion.

For email requests, we will explain any records we must retain before completing deletion. Data stored on your own machines or in external services, such as repository and agent providers, may need to be removed separately.

Uninstalling the mobile app or switching off notifications does not delete your account.
